# Project Freedom Roadmap

## Product mission

Helping people understand their financial position, track their progress and make work optional.

## Completed

- v0.0.9 — Modular dashboard foundation
- v0.1.0 — Financial Insights
- v0.1.1 — Freedom Lifestyle Planner
- v0.1.2 — Monthly Freedom Check-In
- v0.3.1 — Assets, Debts & Overview Completion
- v0.3.2 — Progress History & Monthly Momentum
- v0.4.0 — First Real Product Experience

## Next

- Debt reduction tracking
- Scenario planning
- Improved financial coaching insights

## Later

- User accounts
- Secure cloud persistence
- Mobile optimisation
- Annual Freedom Review

## Product principles

- Explain what financial figures mean
- Support different personal circumstances
- Keep assumptions transparent
- Avoid presenting estimates as guarantees
- Protect user privacy
- Prefer clear guidance over unnecessary complexity
